R6 VOICE -- records your mic during practice for the team's comms review
==========================================================================

What it's for
  Discord mixes everyone together, so the review can't tell who said what.
  If each of us records our own mic, every callout gets the right name and
  lines up with the kill feed -- who called what, who got talked over, who
  died right after a warning.

Setup (once)
  1. Put R6Voice.exe anywhere (Desktop is fine) and double-click it.
     Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't
     signed -- click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  2. Type your in-game (Ubisoft) name exactly as it shows in the kill feed.
  3. Pick your microphone -- the same one Discord uses.
  4. Make sure Windows' clock is set automatically
     (Settings > Time & language > Date & time > "Set time automatically").

Every practice
  - Press "Start recording" when practice starts, "Stop recording" when it
    ends. That's it. It uploads by itself as it goes (every 5 minutes); if
    your internet drops, it catches up later -- even after a restart.
  - "Pause" records silence until you press "Resume".

Privacy
  - It only ever records your own mic -- nobody else's audio.
  - Only what you say while you're actually talking in Discord is used.
    Anything your mic hears while you're muted (or not holding push-to-talk)
    is thrown away on the server automatically.
  - The recordings go only to our team's own server.
