R6 COMPANION -- records your mic at practice for the team's comms review
========================================================================

Each practice
  1. Plug in this USB stick.
  2. Open the R6Companion folder and double-click R6Companion.exe.
  3. That's it. Leave it open (you can minimise it). It starts and stops
     recording by itself when the main app starts and stops the session,
     and uploads your audio after practice.

First time only
  - Type your in-game (Ubisoft) name exactly as it shows in the kill feed,
    and press Save.
  - Once OBS has started (a few seconds), pick the microphone you use for
    Discord and press Use.
  - If Windows asks whether OBS may communicate on networks, you can press
    Cancel -- it only talks to R6 Companion on this PC.

Good to know
  - It only records your microphone -- no screen, no one else's audio.
    (OBS insists on a video track, so the file has a black 640x360 picture.)
  - "Start now" / "Stop now" work any time; "Follow team" goes back to
    starting and stopping with the main app.
  - If the internet drops, it keeps recording; uploads catch up later, even
    after closing the app.
  - Closing the window stops your recording.
